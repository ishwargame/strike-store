Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

1. What We Collect

To run the store we collect only what is necessary: your in-game username, the items you purchase, and a payment reference ID. We never ask for your Minecraft account password, and you should never share it with anyone.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to deliver your purchases, verify transactions, provide support and keep the network secure. We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

3. Sharing

Payment details are processed by our payment providers; we only store the reference needed to confirm your purchase. Data is shared with third parties only when required by law.

4. Data Retention

Purchase records are kept for as long as your account exists on the network, plus the time required for accounting and dispute handling. Support tickets are closed after resolution and archived.

5. Your Rights

You may ask us at any time which data we hold about you and request correction or deletion, subject to legal record-keeping requirements. Send requests through a Discord ticket.

6. Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to the Strike Network team via our Discord server. We aim to respond to all privacy requests within 7 days.
